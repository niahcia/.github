# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

NIAHCIA contributors are expected to collaborate professionally and respectfully.

We welcome technical disagreement. Critique ideas, code, protocol designs, measurements, and assumptions directly without attacking the people presenting them.

## Expected behavior

- communicate clearly and respectfully
- give technical criticism with useful context
- accept correction when evidence changes
- document important decisions
- avoid harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks
- respect privacy and responsible security disclosure

## Unacceptable behavior

- targeted harassment
- discriminatory abuse
- doxxing or sharing private information without permission
- threats or intimidation
- deliberately disruptive behavior
- knowingly falsifying test or benchmark evidence

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove comments, close discussions, restrict participation, or ban contributors when necessary to protect productive collaboration.

## Scope

This applies to NIAHCIA repositories, discussions, issue trackers, pull requests, and official project spaces.
